# Brag plan: Orbit

## Rubric
1. **What is it?** Orbit is an internal agency workspace. It holds projects, clusters, tasks, review queues, task requests, time logs, a calendar, messages, a knowledge base, credentials and an AI assistant ("Ask Orbit").
2. **Who is it for?** An agency's managers (admin side) and members.
3. **Hero feature?** The assistant knows the agency's live data. Its quick prompts include "What should I do first?", "Team workload", "Write my standup" and "Rewrite for client: Manglish → polished English".
4. **Most surprising detail?** A one-click Manglish-to-client-English rewrite. Nobody else ships that.
5. **Real copy to use:** "Welcome back / Sign in to your workspace", nav labels (Dashboard, Calendar, Messages, Projects, In Review, Requested Tasks, Members, Time Log, Member Activity), the assistant chips, and the footer "Orbit — Internal use only".
6. **Brand:** indigo #6366f1 → violet #8b5cf6 gradient, ink #1e2047, background #f4f6fb, Inter plus Space Mono, and the hexagon mark ⬡.
7. **Tone:** `default` with a polished finish: playful, clean, on-brand light UI.
8. **Format:** landscape 1920×1080, about 20 s.
9. **Punchline:** "Internal use only. Externally impressive."

## Audio
Original synthesized bed (`composition/assets/orbit-bed.wav`, made by a stdlib Python synth): 120 BPM, Cmaj7–Am7–Fmaj7–G6. The pad and pluck arpeggio play throughout, the kick enters at the ORBIT reveal (5.0 s), claps and hats come in at the task flow (7.4 s), and the drums drop out for the outro (18.8 s). Hits: sparkle on each chip press, a boom on the logo reveals, whoosh and tick on each task-card move. Mastered to about -14.6 LUFS with a 1.5 s fade-out. Final cut is 22.5 s.
